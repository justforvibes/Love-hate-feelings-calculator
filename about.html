Who We Are

JustForVibes makes free, simple web tools built to make you smile, start a debate, or give you something fun to send to your group chat. No sign-up, no clutter.

"100% fun, 0% science. Take the numbers with a pinch of salt and a lot of humor."

The Feelings Calculator

Type two names, pick an emotion such as love, crush, hate, friendship, jealousy or obsession, and get a percentage. You can also create a custom feeling. Same names always give the same result, which makes comparing results with friends more fun.

GTA 6 Edition

We added a GTA 6 Edition with presets like Jason and Lucia love percentage, Bonnie and Clyde compatibility and a GTA 6 hype meter. It is an unofficial fan-made feature for entertainment and is not affiliated with Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive.
